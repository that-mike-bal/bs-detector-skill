# Onboarding Relaunch: Q1 Plan

*Fictional example document for bs-detector. The company, people and numbers are made up.*

**To:** Leadership team, Ledgerly
**From:** Growth
**Decision needed:** Approve the Q1 onboarding relaunch

## Summary

Ledgerly is the only bookkeeping app built for freelancers. Our biggest problem is activation: only 22% of new signups connect a bank account in their first week, and customers who connect a bank account are 3x more likely to still be paying after six months. Connecting a bank account is what keeps customers.

We will relaunch onboarding in Q1 with a new five-email sequence and an in-app checklist. The new sequence will lift first-week activation from 22% to 40% by the end of Q2.

## What we know

- **Signups:** about 9,000 per month.
- **Email:** 31% of new signups open the first onboarding email.
- **Best cohort:** our highest-activating cohort ever (the October 2025 webinar cohort) reached 35% activation.
- **Customers love the checklist idea.** NPS rose from 38 to 47 after we previewed the checklist in the September newsletter.
- **Activation today:** 22% (see summary). Activation for the most recent full month was 24%.

## The plan

1. **Weeks 1–3:** write and design the five-email sequence.
2. **Weeks 2–6:** build the in-app checklist. Engineering will pick this up once the billing migration ships.
3. **Week 7:** launch to 100% of new signups.
4. **Weeks 8–12:** measure and iterate.

## Impact

Moving activation from 22% to 40% means roughly 1,600 more activated customers every month (9,000 × 18%). At our average of $19 per month, that's about $365K in new annual recurring revenue (ARR) per monthly cohort.

## Ask

Approve the relaunch so we can start in January. We'll share final costs once the design work is scoped.
