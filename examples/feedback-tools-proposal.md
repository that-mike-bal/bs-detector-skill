# Proposal: Consolidating Our Customer Feedback Tools

*Prepared for the Leadership Team · Fieldnote (fictional company)*

## Introduction

In today's rapidly evolving customer landscape, understanding the voice of the customer has never been more important. Organizations that harness customer feedback effectively are better positioned to drive meaningful outcomes, foster loyalty, and unlock sustainable growth. This proposal outlines a holistic approach to how Fieldnote captures, analyzes, and acts on customer feedback.

## Background and purpose of this document

As requested by the Leadership Team at the September planning session, this document reviews the customer feedback tools we use today and makes a recommendation on whether to consolidate them. Leadership asked for a recommendation on consolidation, the expected benefits, the cost, and a timeline. This document provides that recommendation, along with the expected benefits, cost, and timeline.

## Current state

Today, customer feedback at Fieldnote lives in four places:

- **Survey tool:** NPS and post-onboarding surveys, owned by Customer Success. About 1,900 responses a quarter.
- **Support desk:** feature requests tagged by agents, owned by Support. About 700 tagged tickets a quarter.
- **Sales notes:** objections and requests logged in the CRM, owned by Sales. Volume unknown, because tagging is inconsistent.
- **Product board:** a public idea board where customers vote, owned by Product. About 300 new ideas a quarter.

Because feedback lives in four places, no one has a complete picture of what customers are asking for. Product managers spend time pulling feedback from four systems. Feedback that lives in four separate systems is hard to bring together, and the fact that it is spread across four tools means teams often miss requests that appear in a tool they don't check.

## The opportunity

Consolidating feedback into a single platform would give every team one place to see what customers want. A single source of truth would empower product, support, sales and success to collaborate seamlessly and make better decisions, faster.

We estimate consolidation will save the product team 40% of the time they currently spend on research.

## Options considered

1. **Keep things as they are.** No cost, no disruption, but the fragmentation remains.
2. **Consolidate into the product board.** The board already exists and customers know it. It can import support tickets but not surveys or CRM notes.
3. **Adopt a dedicated feedback platform.** A dedicated platform can ingest all four sources, de-duplicate requests, and link them to revenue. Annual cost for our size is about $24,000.

## Dependencies

We cannot begin the rollout until IT finishes the single sign-on migration, because all new tools are required to use SSO. IT expects to finish the migration at the end of Q2, so the earliest start is Q3.

## Timeline

- **Q3:** Select a vendor, sign the contract, connect the four sources.
- **Q4:** Train teams and retire the separate tagging workflows.

## Conclusion

In conclusion, consolidating our feedback tools represents a significant opportunity to strengthen our customer-centric culture and position Fieldnote for long-term success. By bringing feedback together in one place, we can ensure that every voice is heard and every decision is informed.

We recommend adopting a dedicated feedback platform (option 3) at about $24,000 a year, starting in Q3.
