# Pro Pricing Review

## Market context
Prices across our category rose in the last 18 months. Two of our three closest competitors raised their mid-tier plans, one by 15% and one by 20%. The third held prices but cut included storage.

## What customers told us
We surveyed 412 Pro customers in August. Most rated value for money as "good" or "very good". The features they valued most were shared workspaces and the audit log, and only 9% said price was their top concern.

## Our pricing history
Pro has been $39 per seat since 2023. Since then we've added shared workspaces, the audit log and single sign-on to the plan without changing the price.

## Expected impact
Based on our churn data from the 2023 change, we model a small loss of seats against a larger gain in revenue.

## Recommendation
We recommend raising Pro from $39 to $49 per seat in Q3. We expect to lose about 4% of Pro seats and gain about $1.2M in annual revenue.
