# Next Year's Initiatives

## Self-serve onboarding
Rebuilding onboarding so new teams can set up without a sales call. This could lift trial conversion and cut sales cost on small accounts. It would take most of the growth team for two quarters.

## Enterprise permissions
Adding role-based permissions and audit controls that larger customers ask for. This could unlock several stalled enterprise deals. It needs the platform team and a security review.

## Mobile app
A mobile app for reviewing and approving work on the go. Customers mention it in surveys, though usage data shows most work happens at a desk. It would mean hiring two mobile engineers.

## Partner integrations
Native integrations with the three tools customers most often connect to. This could reduce churn among teams that rely on manual exports. Each integration takes about six weeks.

We look forward to discussing these options.
