# Recommendation: Renew the Support Desk Contract for Two Years

**Ask:** Approve a two-year renewal of our support desk software at $38,400 per year, signed by March 15. The current contract ends March 31.

**Why renew:** We handle about 6,000 tickets a month on it. Median first response is 2 hours, down from 5 when we switched in 2024. Moving now would mean migrating three years of ticket history during our busiest quarter.

**Why two years:** The vendor offers 12% off the one-year price of $43,600 for a two-year term. That saves $10,400 over the term.

**What we checked:** We priced two alternatives. Both came in within 5% of this price, and neither imports our macros or reporting.

**Risk:** We're committed for two years. The contract lets us drop up to 20% of seats at the one-year mark if headcount falls.

**Owner:** Jordan Lee, Support Operations.
