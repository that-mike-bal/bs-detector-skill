# Proposal: Plain-Text Onboarding Emails

Our onboarding sequence is five HTML emails sent over the first two weeks. Trial-to-paid conversion has been between 4.1% and 4.6% for the last six quarters.

We tested plain-text versions with a pilot group of five new customers last month. Four of the five opened every email.

This proves that plain text works. Switching the whole sequence to plain text will double trial-to-paid conversion, to over 9%, within two weeks of launch.

The change is small: rewrite five emails and swap the templates. Engineering estimates two days.

Recommendation: switch all onboarding emails to plain text next sprint.
