# Pricing: Where We Are

Over the past two quarters, pricing has come up in several conversations across sales, support and finance. Sales has heard from prospects that our Pro tier feels expensive next to two newer competitors. Support sees a steady stream of questions about how seats are counted on annual plans. Finance has noted that discounting has crept up, with the average discount on new deals rising from 12% to 19%.

There are a number of directions we could take. We could simplify seat counting. We could revisit the Pro tier price. We could tighten discount approvals, or we could introduce a usage-based add-on for larger teams.

Each of these has trade-offs. Simplifying seats would cut support load but could reduce expansion revenue. A lower Pro price might help win rates but would compress margin. Tighter discount approvals could slow deals. A usage add-on would take engineering time we've committed elsewhere.

Given all of this, we may want to consider exploring whether some combination of these could potentially be worth looking into further as we plan the rest of the year.
