# Referral Program Launch Plan

Goal: grow referrals from 3% to 8% of new signups by the end of Q2.

## Phase 1 (weeks 1-3): Launch the campaign
Email every active customer their personal referral link, pulled from the new referral dashboard. Track each referrer's shares and conversions in the dashboard, and send a reminder to anyone who hasn't shared after a week.

## Phase 2 (weeks 4-6): Build the referral dashboard
Engineering builds the referral dashboard: personal links, share tracking, and conversion attribution per referrer.

## Phase 3 (weeks 7-8): Rewards
Turn on rewards: one free month for each referred signup that converts to paid.

## Owners
Campaign: Maya (growth). Dashboard: Tom (engineering). Rewards: Priya (billing).

## Success
Referrals at 8% of new signups by the end of Q2, measured in the dashboard.
