## Look for

- A long doc driving a small or single decision.
- Appendix-grade detail in the body.
- A reading time the stated audience won't give it, such as a 40-minute doc for an exec who has five.

## How to tell

Ask how long this job needs. A decision with one ask usually fits a page or two; a plan with owners and dates, perhaps five. Compare that with `pages` and `reading_minutes`, and put both numbers in the readout's Length line.

## Not this

A reference spec, contract or runbook, where the length is the content. A long but tight doc: judge it by its job, not by a word count.

## When it's worst

- **Stops it:** the audience won't read it at this length, so the decision won't happen.
- **Costs trust:** it's two or three times longer than the job needs.
- **Slows the reader:** somewhat long, but tightly written.

## How it reads

"Sixty pages for a pricing decision. This job needs five. Lead with the call, the numbers and the risk, and move the rest to an appendix."
