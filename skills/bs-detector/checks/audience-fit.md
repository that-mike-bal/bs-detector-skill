## Look for

- Jargon or acronyms the stated audience won't know.
- The obvious first question this audience will ask, left unanswered: cost, owner, risk, or how success is measured.
- The wrong altitude: implementation detail for executives, or strategy for the team doing the work.
- Bad news buried or softened, such as "came in below expectations" for a 30% miss.
- No stakes this audience will recognize as theirs.

## How to tell

Picture the least-briefed person in the room reading it cold. What's their first question, and does the doc answer it? Where do they stop reading?

## Not this

Terms this audience uses every day. A tone you'd have chosen differently.

## When it's worst

- **Stops it:** it asks this reader for a decision they can't make, or it leaves out the one thing they'll ask first, which is usually the cost.
- **Costs trust:** bad news is softened, or it's pitched at the wrong altitude.
- **Slows the reader:** an undefined term or two.

## How it reads

"Someone in the room will ask what this costs, and you don't say. Put the number in the first paragraph."
