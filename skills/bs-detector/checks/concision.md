## Look for

- **Over-explaining:** defining what this audience already knows, walking through obvious steps, justifying choices nobody questioned.
- **Repetition:** the same point in the summary, the body and the close, or the same phrase three times.
- **Handed-back context:** restating the brief, the request or background the reader supplied. In reviewer mode, compare against the brief.
- **Throat-clearing:** paragraphs of setup before anything is claimed.

## How to tell

Delete the passage in your head. If the reader loses nothing, it's bloat. A `repeated_sentence_share` above 0.1, or a phrase in `repeated_phrases`, is evidence of repetition; a reread confirms it. For handed-back context, quote the brief's line and the doc's line side by side.

## Not this

A summary at the top of a long doc that the body expands. Restating the ask at the end of a long doc. Long sentences that are still clear.

## When it's worst

- **Costs trust:** a section restates the brief to the person who wrote it, or most of the doc is restatement.
- **Slows the reader:** a passage could lose half its words and nothing would be lost.

Concision alone rarely stops a doc. When bloat buries the point, report it under the point check.

## How it reads

"Section 2 hands my brief back to me. I wrote it. Cut it and start with what's new."
