---
name: support
summary: Claims the doc's own content, narrative or data doesn't back, and claims that contradict each other.
signals: none
---

## Look for

- A number with nothing behind it: "a 40% lift" with no baseline, source or working.
- Figures that don't reconcile: parts that don't sum, or the same figure with different values in two places.
- A forecast that the doc's own history can't reach.
- Correlation stated as cause, or five interviews presented as "customers want".
- A conclusion that doesn't follow from the evidence the doc gives.

## How to tell

Recompute from the doc's own figures, and trace each claim the ask depends on to something in the doc. Never supply your own numbers, and never treat silence elsewhere as a contradiction.

## Not this

A claim the audience already accepts without proof. A figure with a named source you can't open: label that "worth verifying", not unsupported.

## When it's worst

- **Stops it:** the doc's own numbers can't reach the claim the ask rests on, or two key figures contradict each other.
- **Costs trust:** a claim the ask depends on has nothing behind it, or an inference is stated as fact.
- **Slows the reader:** a minor figure has no source.

## How it reads

"I can't make your numbers work. Page 1 says 20% opt in, and your best campaign converted 40%. Reaching 30% overall needs 90% of opt-ins. Show the working or change the target."
