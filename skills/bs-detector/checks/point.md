## Look for

- No ask, recommendation or decision anywhere, only context and options.
- Several initiatives with nothing saying which one matters.
- An ask that arrives late, after background the reader didn't need first.
- A thesis that could open any doc: "outlines our approach to the evolving landscape."

## How to tell

Write the point in one line from memory before rereading. If you can't, the reader can't either. Then find the first sentence that states the ask. An `ask_position` above 0.3 means it sits more than a third of the way in.

## Not this

A reference doc, FAQ or status log with no decision to drive. Judge those on whether the reader can find what they came for.

## When it's worst

- **Stops it:** there's no recoverable point, or the decision-maker would have to guess what you want. This leads the readout.
- **Costs trust:** the point is there, but it's buried past the first screen or split across initiatives with no priority.
- **Slows the reader:** the ask is findable but soft ("we may want to consider").

## How it reads

"Nine pages and I still can't tell you what you want. Put the ask in the first line: [the decision], by [date]."
